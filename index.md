# Privacy Policy – Evola Exchange

**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 16, 2026

Evola Exchange ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use Evola Exchange.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Evola Exchange may collect or process certain information through the App and its integrated third-party services.

### A. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following information locally:

- Favorite currencies.
- Recent currency conversions.
- App preferences and settings.
- Cached exchange rates.

This information is used to provide the App's functionality and improve your experience.

### B. Automatically Collected Information

Depending on the enabled SDK features and configuration, the App's third-party services may collect:

- Device information, including device model and operating system.
- IP address and approximate location derived from IP address.
- Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers.
- App usage and interaction information.
- App performance and diagnostic information.
- Advertising interaction and performance information.

We do not require users to create an account to use the App's core currency conversion features.

AdMob may collect and process certain information, including:

Crashlytics may collect:

- Crash timestamps.
- Technical diagnostic information.

For more information:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Evola Exchange may connect to third-party exchange rate providers to retrieve current or updated currency exchange rates.

Currency codes and technical request information may be transmitted to these providers.

Exchange rate providers may process technical information, including IP addresses, according to their own privacy policies.
